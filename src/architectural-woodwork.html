Architectural woodwork & paneling · DC · MD · VA

The room is furniture too.

Architectural woodwork restoration for Washington DC, Maryland and Virginia — interior wood walls and paneling, lobby woodwork, wainscot, built-ins, handrails and historic trim, brought back with the same hand methods we give fine furniture. In our shop when the piece can travel; on site when it can’t.

01 — On SiteOctober 2016 · Pennsylvania Avenue

The proof

The bench went to the building.

In October 2016 the workshop refinished the wooden lobby walls of a Pennsylvania Avenue address — architectural woodwork, brought to the same standard as the furniture. The same patience, the same hand methods, applied at the scale of a room rather than a piece.

That is the difference a furniture workshop brings to architectural work. Most contractors who refinish paneling are painters working in wood; we are furniture restorers working at architectural scale. Color is matched the way we match a tabletop. Grain is respected the way we respect a veneer. And the standard at the end is the one a cabinetmaker would sign, not the one a punch list would accept.

Wherever the woodwork can be removed and brought to the shop — doors, panels, built-in sections, shutters — it gets the full bench treatment under one roof. What cannot travel is worked in place, planned around how the space is used.

† A full case study of the lobby project — with photographs — is being assembled from the shop’s records.

02 — The ScopeWalls · Wainscot · Built-ins · Rails

What we take on

If it’s fine woodwork, it qualifies.

The line between furniture and architecture is thinner than it looks — the same species, the same joinery, the same finishes, fixed to the wall instead of standing on the floor.

  • Interior wood walls and full-height paneling
  • Wainscot, boardroom and library paneling
  • Lobby and common-area woodwork
  • Built-ins, bookcases and window seats
  • Handrails, newels and stair parts
  • Historic trim, casings and mantels
  • Kitchen cabinets — the same hand methods, at cabinet scale
  • Entry and interior doors — a specialty of the shop, with its own page
  • In the shop
    Anything that can be removed — doors, shutters, panel sections, built-in components — travels to Sterling for the full bench treatment: hand stripping, repair, color, finish.
  • On site
    Fixed woodwork is refinished in place, planned around how the space is used — the lobby on Pennsylvania Avenue was done exactly this way.
  • The standard
    Never acids, never dipping, never high-pressure water. Color matched on the wood itself, finishes chosen for how the surface will actually live.
  • Who hires us
    Building and property managers, institutions, designers, general contractors needing a specialty finisher, and homeowners with a paneled room worth doing properly.
03 — At Account ScaleBuildings · Institutions · The trade

Larger engagements

One room, or the whole building.

Architectural work rarely arrives alone. The same account often carries the furniture in the room, the doors on the corridor, and the paneling behind them.

For hotels, clubs, offices and institutions we run this as a single engagement — commercial furniture restoration and architectural woodwork under one accountable name, documented in photographs at every stage. Designers and architects will find the workroom’s trade terms on For the Trade.
