Case study · Private residential · Antique restoration

The Chippendale secretary.

It came in blackened — decades of darkened finish and grime hiding everything down to the ball-and-claw feet. What left the shop was a piece of glowing figured mahogany that, in all likelihood, no one living had ever seen.

The turn

The case gave up its secret.

Stripped by hand and partly disassembled on the bench, the secretary revealed figured mahogany under all that darkness — the kind of grain a maker saves for a piece meant to be looked at for two hundred years.

This is why the shop strips by hand and never in a tank: a piece like this survives on its details. The joints were re-glued, the case refinished and polished, and the secretary went home glowing — where it was photographed on the client’s own floor, which is where after-photographs belong.
