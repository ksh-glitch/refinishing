The visual archive · DC · MD · VA

The plates.

Since 1991 the shop has kept one habit without fail: every piece is photographed three times — the day it arrives, stripped by hand to bare wood, and fully restored. No staging, no retouching. What follows is that before-and-after record of furniture restoration, kept for heirlooms from across Washington DC, Maryland and Virginia.

01 — The Three-Step ShowcaseAs received · Stripped · Restored

The shop tradition

Three photographs, every piece.

The middle photograph is the honest one. Stripped to bare wood, a piece hides nothing, and neither do we. It is the step that decides whether a finish lasts a season or a generation.
