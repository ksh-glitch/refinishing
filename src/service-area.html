Service area · Pick-up & delivery

Where the truck goes.

The workshop is in Sterling, Virginia — Loudoun County, near Dulles — and the truck goes where the furniture lives: across Washington DC, Northern Virginia and Maryland, with pick-up and delivery quoted alongside every estimate. Heirlooms have reached us from across the country.

01 — The RoundsDC · Northern Virginia · Maryland

How it works

Both ends of the job, handled.

We collect the piece from your home or site and return it restored — our own pick-up and delivery, not a courier. The cost is quoted with your estimate, so there are no surprises at either door.

Washington, DC

The District

From Georgetown to Capitol Hill, including the historic districts where our door restorations live.

Northern Virginia

NoVA

Fairfax, Arlington, Alexandria, McLean, Great Falls, Reston and Leesburg, with Loudoun, Fairfax, Prince William and Fauquier Counties on the regular rounds.

Maryland

The Maryland side

Bethesda and Potomac through Montgomery County — plus Frederick, Baltimore, Prince George’s and Charles Counties.

Beyond the DMV

Across the country

For pieces that justify the trip, clients ship to the workshop from across the US — the guide is Shipping an Heirloom.

02 — The WorkshopSterling, Virginia · near Dulles

Come see the bench

Call first, then come by.

Clients are welcome at the shop — most memorably at stain selection, when you choose the color on your own stripped piece, sample by sample.

We’re in and out for pick-ups and deliveries, so please call before stopping by. And if the trip to Sterling doesn’t suit, we match stain from photographs of the furniture your piece needs to live alongside.
